## What changed

In 2026.9, Speckle moved to a new data model that handles much larger projects and works faster.
Your models are organized in a way that's built for scale, and that
also lets newer features — including Speckle Intelligence — understand how parts of your model
relate to each other, not just what each part is. See [Ask questions about your models with Speckle
Intelligence](/analytics/intelligence).

For everything else that is new in 2026.9, see [What's new](/whats-new/users).

## What stayed the same

How you work day to day stays the same. Publish and load through your connectors, view and share in
the web app, exactly as before, and faster on the new data model.
Models you already published stay readable.

## Why we changed it

Bigger projects were slowing the old format down. The new one is built to hold much larger
models without that penalty, and it opens the door to querying and understanding your data in ways
the old format couldn't support — including how Speckle Intelligence works out what connects to
what.

## Who should pay closer attention

Most people only need to update their connectors. Two cases need more care.

**Navisworks.** There is no 2026.9 Navisworks desktop connector. **2026.8** is the last one, and it
remains the advised path for a publish around **200 MB**. Upload larger NWC and NWD files instead.
See [Navisworks](/connectors/navisworks).

**ACC sync and file uploads.** These write the new data format, which can break a script or report
that expects the old one. **Compatibility mode** keeps them in the old format until **1 November
2026**; see [Compatibility mode](/it-admin/compatibility-mode).

## What you should do now

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update your connectors">
    The upgrade window is open until **1 November 2026**. Update together with the people you share
    models with: a connector from 2026.8 or earlier cannot load a version published by a 2026.9
    connector. Skip Navisworks, which has no 2026.9 connector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flag it if you rely on ACC sync or file uploads">
    If something downstream depends on the old data format, ask us to switch your workspace into
    compatibility mode now. Update the downstream tool before **1 November 2026**, when
    compatibility mode ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>
