## What is a workspace?

A workspace is your team's operating area in Speckle.
Use it to manage projects, assign access, and keep delivery work under one shared security model.
Use this page as a quick map of where to go next.

The workspace home page gives a fast health check through summary cards and recent activity surfaces.
Use it as a quick check page, not a full management page.

Workspace members can view settings pages, but only workspace admins can change workspace-level controls.
For support-session workflows on self-hosted enterprise deployments, see [Admin Support Mode](/workspaces/admin-support).
