## What is Speckle?

Speckle connects design tools so you can publish, view, share, and analyze model data with your team.
Use connectors to publish from your design software, or upload a supported file to view it in your browser.

## Why use Speckle?

- **Exchange model data** between tools such as Revit, Rhino, Grasshopper, and Power BI.
- **Review models together** in the web viewer, with version history and comments.
- **Analyze properties** with dashboards or your own scripts.
- **Build integrations** with Speckle's APIs and SDKs.

## How Speckle works

Connectors are plugins for your design software. They publish geometry and properties to Speckle,
where your team can view the model and work with its data.

## What you can do with Speckle

- **Exchange designs:** share selected model data across disciplines.
- **Analyze models:** ask [Speckle Intelligence](/analytics/intelligence) about your models, or use
  [Intelligence Dashboards](/analytics/intelligence-dashboards) or the Power BI connector.
- **Review models:** share a viewer link, raise issues, and discuss objects with your team.
- **Build custom tools:** use the APIs and SDKs to create apps, integrations, and automations.
