## Grasshopper

### Template Files
- [Grasshopper Template File](../static/gh/speckle-grasshopper-template.gh) - Basic template with common Speckle nodes and workflows

### Sample Files
Sample files are installed with your Grasshopper connector. Navigate to **File** > **Special Folders** > **Components Folder** > **Speckle Sample Files** in Grasshopper to find them.
