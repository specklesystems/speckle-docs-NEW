<Accordion title="Why does my model have the wrong colors in the web browser viewer?">
  The 3D viewer does not draw some colors and materials yet, so affected objects show as flat grey.
  Your published data keeps the colors. If the model was published before your project moved to the
  2026.9 data format, publish it again to restore them. For the known cases, see [3D
  viewer](/3d-viewer/introduction). If a color is still missing, let us know in our [Community
  Forum](https://speckle.community/c/help).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why are some objects that I published missing from my model?">
  After you publish a Speckle model, click on the **Report** button to see any errors that may have
  occurred. Click on any item in the report to highlight that item in your application. Some objects
  may not be supported for publishing.
</Accordion>
