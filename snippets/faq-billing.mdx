        You can purchase our [paid plan](https://www.speckle.systems/pricing) directly from the web app.

    <Accordion title="How do I add more users to my workspace?">
        Workspace admins can invite users from Settings -\> People. Each plan includes capacity for a certain number of team members. If you need to add more users than your plan allows, contact us to discuss upgrading to Enterprise, which supports unlimited users (both editors and viewers).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What happens if I exceed my plan's user limit?">
        Existing users remain in the workspace, but you won't be able to add new users until you upgrade your plan. Contact us at hello@speckle.systems to discuss upgrading options that better fit your team size.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Do Guests count toward my user limit?">
        Yes, both workspace Members and Guests count toward your user limit. The limit applies to the total number of users who have access to your workspace, regardless of their role. This means if you invite external collaborators as Guests, they count toward your limit just like internal team Members do.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What does 'anticipated syncs' mean on my plan?">
        Anticipated syncs reflect typical monthly activity for your plan tier, including model version creation and productive receives. These numbers help you understand expected usage patterns, but they're guidance rather than hard limits. Nothing stops automatically if you exceed them—we understand that coordination needs can spike during active project periods.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What happens if my workspace exceeds the anticipated sync usage?">
        Nothing breaks or stops working. Anticipated syncs are guidance for expected usage, not real-time enforcement. If your usage consistently exceeds the anticipated range over multiple months, we'll reach out to discuss what plan best fits your workflow and coordination needs.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="How do version storage limits work?">
        Each plan includes capacity for storing model versions across all your projects. Versions represent instances of a model at a given time. If you reach your version storage limit, you won't be able to publish additional versions until you add more capacity or upgrade your plan. Contact us at hello@speckle.systems if you're approaching your limit to explore options.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What happens if I exceed my version storage limit?">
        Once you reach your plan's version storage limit, you won't be able to publish new versions to your workspace. Your existing data and versions remain accessible. Contact us at hello@speckle.systems to explore custom engagement options or upgrading to a plan with more capacity.
    </Accordion>
