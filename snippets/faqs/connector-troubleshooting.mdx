    <Accordion title="Why is the Speckle plugin not showing up after I installed the connector?">
    Make sure your version of the application is one of the supported versions. Installation issues are also sometimes caused by conflicting plugins. Report your problem in our [Community Forum](https://speckle.community/c/help) and include:

    - Your application version
    - Your Speckle connector version
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Why did I get an error when using the Speckle command to open the connector?">
    If you still have the legacy version installed, make sure you have `version 2.23` or above. Any version below 2.23 will have conflicting commands with the AutoCAD connector.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Why does my model have the wrong colors in the web browser viewer?">
    In the viewer, select the `View Modes` button in the side bar, and switch the view mode to `Shaded`. If you still don't see your object colors, let us know in our [Community Forum](https://speckle.community/c/help).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Why are some objects that I published or loaded missing from my model?">
    After you publish or load a Speckle model, click on the `Report` button to see any errors that may have occurred. Click on any item in the report to highlight that item in your application. Some objects may not be supported for publishing or loading.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="What should I do if the plugin UI is empty or unresponsive, or if my application crashes?">
    For major issues, you can sometimes find a `log` file in your directory at `AppData\Roaming\Speckle\Logs`. Report your problem in our [Community Forum](https://speckle.community/c/help) and include:

    - Your application version
    - Your Speckle connector version
    - A copy of the log
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Why do I get a certificate verify failed error when I publish or load a model?">
    This usually means your company's VPN, proxy or firewall is inspecting (re-signing) HTTPS traffic to Speckle's file storage, and the connector doesn't trust your company's certificate. Signing in and browsing your projects can still work, because they use a different connection. The error may read `unable to get local issuer certificate`.

    Ask your IT team to exclude the domains listed in [Firewall and network requirements](/it-admin/setup#firewall-and-network-requirements), especially `*.digitaloceanspaces.com`, from HTTPS/SSL inspection. Allowing them alone isn't enough.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How do I uninstall my connector?">
    All Speckle connectors are installed like any other program. You can uninstall them directly from your system's `Applications`. For a full uninstall, also remove `Speckle Desktop Services` if it is installed.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How do I update my connector to the latest version?">
    You can always download the latest version of the connector [in the web app](https://app.speckle.systems/connectors). If a new version of the connector is available, you will also see a notification in your connector UI.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Where do I download Speckle Manager?">
    Speckle Manager has been deprecated and is no longer required to install connectors. It can still be [downloaded and used with legacy connectors](https://releases.speckle.systems/legacy-connectors).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How do I install connectors for Mac?">
    Most connectors are available for Windows only. The Blender and SketchUp connectors also run on macOS: see [Blender on macOS](/connectors/manual-installation/blender#macos-installation) and [SketchUp on macOS](/connectors/manual-installation/sketchup#macos-installation).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Where can I get more help?">
    Post on our [Community Forum](https://speckle.community/). We try to respond as quickly as possible.
    </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
