<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do v3 connectors and legacy connectors support the same tools?">
    They match, but not completely.

    v3 connectors are available for: Revit, Rhino, Archicad, AutoCAD, Navisworks, SketchUp, Blender, Civil 3D, Grasshopper, ETABS, Tekla, and Power BI. These connectors are available in the legacy version as well, but we recommend you use the v3 version.

    These connectors will be available only in the legacy version: Dynamo, Unity, Unreal, Excel, SAP2000, SAFE, CSiBridge, Bentley (MicroStation, OpenRoads, OpenRail, OpenBuilding), Advance Steel, TopSolid and QGIS.

  </Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I still use legacy connectors?">
  No, not any longer. Legacy connectors are no longer supported or updated. Move your workflows to
  v3 connectors: see [Migration steps](/migration/connectors-v2-to-v3#migration-steps).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I update the v3 connectors without Speckle Manager?">
  If a new version of the v3 connectors is available, you'll see a little Update button when you
  open it up in your host application. You can always go back to app.speckle.systems, to check for
  the latest version.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What's happening to Speckle Manager?">
  Speckle Manager is deprecated. It installs only legacy connectors, and v3 connectors do not need
  it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I uninstall the v3 connectors?">
  Just head to your computer's standard programs/applications list and uninstall like any other app.
</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I remove an account or change the default one without Speckle Manager?">
    Accounts can be removed or changed even without Speckle Manager. Whichever account you had selected last time (across devices) is your default for the next session.

    1. Click your profile picture at the top to open your account manager.

    2. Click to Switch your active account. Your last used account will be the default account.

    3. You can add a new account, or click on the Trash icon on any current account to remove it.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to upgrade immediately?">
    Yes. v2 connectors are no longer supported or updated, and there is no guarantee they keep working with the current server. Switch to v3 connectors: see [Migration steps](/migration/connectors-v2-to-v3#migration-steps).
  </Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will my old v2 data disappear?">
  No. v2 streams remain in read-only mode in your personal area. You can still view or download
  them.
</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I roll back to v2?">
    No; v2 and v3 connectors are built on different data models and cannot reliably be used interchangeably.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
