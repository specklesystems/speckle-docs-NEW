<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How can I upgrade from a free plan?">
    You can upgrade your workspace to a paid plan (Team or Enterprise) from Settings -\> Billing. 
    Contact us at hello@speckle.systems if you need help choosing the right plan for your needs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I change a user's project role?">
    Workspace admins and project owners can change user roles on projects. Changing a user's role 
    from **Can edit** to **Can view** limits their ability to publish and load models, but they 
    remain a member of the project and workspace. This does not affect billing—plans are based on 
    the number of users, not their roles.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I remove users from my workspace?">
    Yes, workspace admins can remove users from Settings -\> People. Removing a user does not 
    immediately free up a user slot if you're at your plan's limit—the user count adjusts when 
    you add new users or upgrade your plan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
