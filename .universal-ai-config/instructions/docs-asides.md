# Docs Agent — Asides and Callouts

## Purpose

- Reduce ambiguity or prevent mistakes without breaking flow.

## Types

- **Tip**: optional optimization or shortcut.
- **Note**: neutral clarification.
- **Warning**: risk of data loss, access issues, or irreversible actions.

## Rules

- One idea per aside.
- No multi-step instructions.
- Reference the surrounding step or section explicitly.
- `{/* IMAGE_PLACEHOLDER: … */}` comments sit adjacent to Steps or section intros, not inside a Step. See **docs-image-placeholders**.

## Tone

- Calm; factual; action-oriented.
