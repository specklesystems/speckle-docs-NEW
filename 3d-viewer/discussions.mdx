---
title: Discussions (self‑hosted servers)
description: 'How model feedback works on self‑hosted Speckle servers that do not use workspace Issues.'
---

On Speckle Cloud workspaces (hosted Speckle), model‑centric feedback, review items, and decisions are captured with **Issues** and their replies. See the [Issues](/3d-viewer/issues) page for current, fully supported workflows.

On a **self‑hosted Speckle server** without Issues, a release before 2026.9 may instead show the older **Discussions** and comments experience in the 3D viewer. In that case:

- Discussions act as the primary way to keep feedback tied to specific objects or views.
- Comments inside a discussion thread behave similarly to replies on Issues.

From 2026.9, the web app has no Discussions: model feedback lives in Issues.
