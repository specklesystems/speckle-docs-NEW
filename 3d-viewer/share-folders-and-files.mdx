Share a single folder, file or model when someone needs part of a project but not all of it. You
can give people and groups a role on just that item, restrict a folder so only some people reach
it, or create a view link for people outside your workspace.

## Who can share what

| You want to                                               | Role you need on the item            |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Give people or groups a role on a folder, file or model   | **Owner**                            |
| Restrict a folder or file, or lift the restriction        | **Owner**                            |
| Create a view link to a model                             | **Editor** or **Owner**              |
| Create a view link to a folder or file                    | **Owner**                            |
| See, edit and revoke the links that open an item          | **Owner**                            |
| See and revoke every link in the project or the workspace | Project **Owner** or workspace admin |

Project owners and workspace admins are **Owner** of everything in the project. You can give a role
only up to your own. See [Roles and permissions](/workspaces/roles-and-seats).

You can share with members and guests of the project's workspace, and with its groups. To give
access to someone outside the workspace, invite them to the workspace first, or create a view link.

## Share with people and groups

{/* IMAGE_PLACEHOLDER: Files — share dialog for a folder with the Add people or groups field, role picker, Given here and From folders above sections, the Restricted switch and the Links section. */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share dialog">
    In **Files**, open the menu of a folder, file or model and select **Share…**. On a folder's
    preview, you can also select **Share…** on its **Access** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick who to add">
    In **Add people or groups…**, search for a workspace member, guest or group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a role and add">
    Choose **Owner**, **Editor** or **Viewer** (the default), then select **Add**. They appear under
    **Given here** at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every change in the share dialog saves as you make it. Closing the dialog does not undo anything.

The dialog lists everyone who can open the item, in three sections:

- **Given here**: people and groups given this item itself. You can change these.
- **From folders above**: people and groups given a folder this item is in. Change them on that
  folder.
- **Everyone on the project**, or **Owners and admins** when the item is restricted: people who
  reach it through the project or the workspace.

The person who created the item shows as **Created it**. They are its **Owner**, and that row cannot
change.

### Change or remove someone's role

Under **Given here**, open the role menu on a person or group. Pick another role, or select
**Remove**. Removing a role here does not remove access given on a folder above or on the project.

## Restrict a folder or file

A folder or file is open to everyone who can open the folder it is in. Restrict it when only some
people should reach it, such as a commercial or HR folder inside a shared project.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share dialog">In **Files**, select **Share…** on the folder or file.</Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Restricted">
    Turn on **Restricted**. The bottom section changes to **Owners and admins**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the people who need it">
    Add the people and groups who should keep access. They appear under **Given here**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A restricted folder reaches only the people and groups given access there or inside it, plus project
owners and workspace admins. Roles given on the project or on a folder above stop at it. To check,
open the folder in **Files**: its **Access** card shows **Restricted** and who reaches it.

<Warning>
  Restricting a folder removes access for everyone who reached it only through the project or a
  folder above, except project owners and workspace admins. Add the people who still need it before
  or right after you restrict it.
</Warning>

Turning **Restricted** off opens the item to everyone who can open the folder it is in again. Every
role given on it and inside it stays.

## Create a view link

A view link lets anyone who has it view the item without signing in. Links you create in the web app
give the **Viewer** role.

{/* IMAGE_PLACEHOLDER: Share dialog — Links section with Options open showing Expires at and Password protect, the Create view link button, and a new link with Copy link. */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share dialog">
    In **Files**, select **Share…** on the folder, file or model. You should see the **Links**
    section at the bottom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set options">
    To add an expiry date or a password, select **Options**, then set **Expires at** or turn on
    **Password protect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and copy the link">
    Select **Create view link**, then **Copy link**. The full address shows only this once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Editors create model links from the viewer instead: open the model and select **Share** → **Share
model…**. The dialog has the same **Links** section. See [Share your
models](/3d-viewer/sharing#share-a-link).

A link to a folder opens it in **Files**. A link to a single file opens **Files** with that file
listed. To embed a model in another site, see [Embed the 3D
viewer](/3d-viewer/sharing#embed-the-3d-viewer).

<Tip>If you lose a link's address, create a new link and revoke the old one.</Tip>

## Edit or revoke a link

The **Links** section of the share dialog lists the links that open the item, with their role, the
last characters of their address and their expiry date. A lock icon marks a link with a password.

- **Edit**: select the pencil icon to change the **Label** or **Expires at** date, or select **Never
  expires**.
- **Revoke**: select the **×** icon. Anyone using the link loses access at once.

A link belongs to the item it opens, not to the person who made it. It keeps working if its creator
leaves the project, until an owner or admin revokes it or it expires.

### See every link in a project or workspace

{/* IMAGE_PLACEHOLDER: Project Settings → Links — table with Link, Kind, Opens, Role, Expires, Last used and Created by columns, and the Danger zone card with Revoke all. */}

- Project owners and workspace admins open project **Settings** → **Links**: every link that opens
  the project or anything in it.
- Workspace admins open workspace **Settings** → **Links**: every link in the workspace.

Both pages list links to models, folders, files, embeds, dashboards, reports and chats, with
**Kind**, **Opens**, **Role**, **Expires**, **Last used** and **Created by**. From a link's menu you
can **Go to what it opens**, **View activity** (version loads made through the link), **Edit…** or
**Revoke…**. **Revoke all**, under **Danger zone**, stops every listed link at once and cannot be
undone.

## When a project becomes private

Changing a project's visibility does not revoke its links on its own. When you change visibility in
project **Settings**, the confirmation asks whether to revoke them. Select **Revoke every link to
this project and what is in it** to stop every link to the project, its folders, files and models.
Leave it clear to keep the links working.

The option appears only to people who can manage the project's links. You can also revoke links
later in project **Settings** → **Links**.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is Share… greyed out on a folder?">
    Sharing a folder or file with people needs the **Owner** role on it. Hover over **Share…** to
    see the reason. Ask a project owner to share it, or to give you **Owner** on that folder.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do people given a folder see the rest of the project?">
    No. They see the project in their list, but only the folders and files they can reach.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add a password to an existing link?">
    No. You can change a link's label and expiry date only. Create a new link with **Password
    protect** on, then revoke the old one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does restricting a folder revoke its links?">
    No. Links to the folder, or to items inside it, keep working. Revoke them in the share dialog or
    in project **Settings** → **Links**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
