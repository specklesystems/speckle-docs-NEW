**Sketch** draws on the model in 3D. Circle problem areas, add arrows to show where things should
move, or write short notes. The ink stays attached to the geometry as you orbit, pan, and zoom.

Sketches are stored on a [saved view](/3d-viewer/saved-views): saving the view keeps the sketch,
and opening that view restores it in place.

<Note>
  Sketch uses the same permission as saving views: the **Contributor** project role or higher.
  Without it, **Sketch** is disabled and its tooltip reads **You don't have write access**.
</Note>

<Note>
  On a self-hosted server, saved views — and so sketches — are available only on Speckle Enterprise
  Server. For deployment setup, see [Enterprise license — Saved
  Views](/developers/server/deployment/enterprise-license#optional-saved-views).
</Note>

## Create a sketch

{/* IMAGE_PLACEHOLDER: Sketch tool — Pen Arrow Revision cloud Text Eraser, colour swatches, Custom color, brush sizes, ink on model geometry in 3D. */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the view">
    Open a model in the 3D viewer and position the camera on the area you want to comment on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Sketch">
    Select **Sketch** on the toolbar, or press Shift+K. The sketch tools appear above the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draw and annotate">
    Pick a tool, colour, and brush size, then draw on the model. You should see the ink stay on the
    geometry when you orbit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the view">
    Select **Save view** on the toolbar, or in **Views**, and name it. The sketch is stored on that
    view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Press Esc to leave the tool. Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes the last stroke.

## Tools

- **Pen** — freehand drawing and circling.
- **Arrow** — point at something.
- **Revision cloud** — mark an area for revision.
- **Text** — place a written note.
- **Eraser** — remove ink.

Preset ink colours are **Red**, **Amber**, **Blue**, **Green**, and **White**. **Custom color**
adds any colour and opacity. Choose a small, medium, or large brush next to the colours.

## Show, hide, and share sketches

Sketches live on their saved views in the **Views** panel. Use the eye control on a view card to
show or hide that view's sketches and measurements. The eye on a group header toggles every view
in that group.

To share a sketch, share its saved view: use **Copy link** on the view card. People who open the
link see the view with its sketch. Private views stay private. See [Saved
views](/3d-viewer/saved-views).

To get an image, select **Screenshot** in the status bar, or **Download image** from **Share**. The
PNG includes sketches and measurements.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is Sketch disabled?">
    Sketch is stored on saved views, so it needs the same permission as **Save view**. Ask a project
    owner for the **Contributor** role, or describe the change in an issue instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do sketches change the model or its geometry?">
    No. Sketches are stored on the saved view. They never modify model geometry or properties.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do unsaved sketches appear for other users?">
    No. Save a view after you draw if you need others to see the ink, or need it later yourself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to a sketch if I delete its saved view?">
    It is deleted with the view. The confirmation dialog says so.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I copy a sketch to the clipboard?">
    No. Download a PNG with **Screenshot** in the status bar, or **Download image** from **Share**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
