The Speckle Community is the best place to ask product questions, share
workflows, showcase cool projects, and learn from other teams using Speckle.
It is also where we share event updates, including where the team will be and
upcoming community standup webinars.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Open the community forum" icon="messages" href="https://speckle.community">
    Visit speckle.community to ask questions, browse existing topics, and join discussions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Get support" icon="life-buoy" href="https://speckle.community/c/help">
    Post issues, troubleshooting questions, or feature requests and get help from the Speckle team
    and community.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What to use it for

- Ask product and workflow questions
- Report bugs and share reproducible examples
- Request features and provide feedback
- Showcase cool projects, experiments, and workflows
- Get notified about events the team will attend and community standup webinars
- Follow product updates and ongoing discussions
