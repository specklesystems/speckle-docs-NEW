and what would make your day-to-day faster.

role and tools. Every study is focused on real project use cases, not synthetic
demos.

## What to expect

- Sessions are usually remote and focused on one workflow at a time.
- We ask about your current process, friction points, and expected outcomes.
- You can join one study or several, depending on relevance.
- Your feedback directly influences priorities, UX, and rollout decisions.
